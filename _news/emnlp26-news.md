How can we use automated novelty judgment of research ideas to accelerate scientific discovery?

In our EMNLP 2026 paper, [Think-Probe-Respond: Improving Large Language Models as Judges of Research Idea Novelty](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.25660), we introduce a lightweight approach that probes LLMs' latent novelty judgments and outperforms strong baselines.
