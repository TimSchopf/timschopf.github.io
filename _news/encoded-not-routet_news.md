Multimodal LLMs are much better at verifying claims from scientific tables than equivalent charts. Our [paper](https://aclanthology.org/2026.findings-aacl.185), accepted to AACL-IJCNLP 2026, investigates why🕵️‍♂️: _do models fail to extract chart information, or fail to use it effectively once extracted?_ Looking forward to sharing our work in Hengqin 🇨🇳!
